## What the mapping changes

1. Start with Reels, script-to-video and chat editing, rather than selecting five pages just because they already exist. They have distinct user starting points and prior demand evidence.
2. Keep Mac, pricing, model access, privacy and export facts as supporting foundations. They help someone evaluate Woven, but their value is not measured only by keyword volume.
3. Treat CapCut comparison copy as a factual refresh first. The current source describes CapCut primarily as a manual/mobile-template tool and labels its file workflow cloud-centric; verify those claims with current sources and a comparable test before expanding them.
4. The current source also claims podcast clipping. A website claim is not proof of working highlight selection, speaker tracking, framing and export. Hold new clipping acquisition content until a real project establishes those details.
5. Several general buyer questions belong on /compare, not a separate page per alternative. Keep /best-ai-video-editor in the existing inventory but defer its expansion until a proper comparative test exists. Do not create a near-duplicate generic roundup on /compare.
6. Industry questions become sections in business, education or product-video pages initially. Split only when a distinct, proven workflow deserves its own example.

## Publishing order

### Foundation: establish a baseline and truthful reference facts

Before changing copy, run the chosen unbranded AI questions in fresh conversations with documented engine/model, web-search settings, date and locale. This planning task does not establish that baseline.

Verify indexing/crawler access and current homepage, pricing, Mac requirements and integrations. Record local versus cloud behavior, export formats, recovery limits and commercial-use terms. The five proposed /docs destinations can begin as documentation sections if there is not enough substance for standalone pages; keep one authoritative answer location per fact.

Do not let a long documentation backlog postpone a demonstrated workflow. Publish only claims whose supporting facts are ready; plainly state relevant limits.

### Wave 2: expand only from demonstrated work

Next candidates: video captions and YouTube Shorts, followed by TikTok, existing footage, getting started and faceless workflows. Refresh CapCut comparison with a fair test. If we want a five-page pilot, the recommended expansion is the first three plus captions and Shorts, provided evidence is ready. Mac remains a foundation page and the CapCut comparison should not outrun its research.

### Wave 3: earned expansion

Generated footage, narration, audio, B-roll, pacing, reusable workflows, branding, business, product ads and education are later evidence-led briefs. Validate demand and fit before each build. Their brainstorm coverage alone is not a reason to publish.

### Hold: validate or explain the limitation

105 questions have a page-level or question-level evidence hold. This does not mean 104 features are missing. It means this repository/content audit did not verify the necessary claim. A good answer may explicitly say that Woven cannot do the requested job and suggest a better fit. Hold only the affected claim, not every supported section on its page.

## Shared demonstration project

Create one original 30-second product Reel using owned or permitted assets. Retain the brief, exact script, scene plan, source assets, recorded commands, revisions and final export. Record app version, model/provider, date, wall-clock time, generation cost and manual corrections. Do not invent benchmarks or treat a provider generation as proof of a finished export.

Reuse evidence appropriately: the Reels page explains the complete result; script-to-video explains sentence-to-scene decisions; chat editing explains selective revision. Avoid republishing the same text under different titles.

## Measurement and expansion decision

Measure product recommendation, passing brand mention and page citation separately. Retain cited sources and factual errors. Track activated trials and paid conversion when attribution is available; do not substitute views or a single favorable answer.

Keep a fixed sample for before/after comparison, including some untouched clusters as a directional comparison group. Repeat prompts to expose variability. This is observational, not a controlled causal experiment: engines change, answers vary, and outside mentions may contribute.

Expand a cluster when repeated relevant recommendations/citations or qualified acquisition signals justify it. If pages are indexed but ignored, inspect cited sources, product fit and evidence quality before adding more near-identical content. Lack of impressions or recommendations in a small sample is inconclusive.
