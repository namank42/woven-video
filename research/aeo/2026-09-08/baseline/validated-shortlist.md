## Evaluation rules

- Freeze wording before testing ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Grok, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode. Keep platform and consumer/API results separate.
- Track recommendation, mention, cited URL, and response presence separately. Record search mode, account state, time and location uncertainty.
- Report the seven core questions separately from the three strategic probes. Q061 is informational; do not interpret no recommendation as a failure.
- Treat Reels, Shorts and TikTok as one broader short-form cluster when interpreting aggregate results; these samples are correlated.
- Captions require a demonstration before a dedicated acquisition page. An existing public claim is not enough to expand support to arbitrary input videos.
- Excluded long-to-short clipping, dubbing, avatars and unsupported feature promises; also excluded generic generation-only keywords despite high volume.
- Q461 names a competitor intentionally but never Woven. The other nine prompts contain no editor brand except Q061 naming ChatGPT.
- Existing 30-question protocol is historical and has not been executed fully. This file is the proposed consumer baseline shortlist; do not silently run the old run.py batch.
