# Woven AEO strategy

Last updated: September 8, 2026. This is the current decision record and starting point. It supersedes earlier measurement proposals where they conflict. No new acquisition pages have been implemented or published as part of this research.

## Objective

Help people evaluating AI video editors discover and choose Woven through useful search and AI answers, particularly for short-form creation, scripts and conversational editing. Measure qualified acquisition and product use alongside visibility. Publishing pages does not guarantee indexing, citations or recommendations.

## Agreed approach

- Keep the 500-question bank as a research and editorial backlog, not 500 landing pages or proven AI prompt demand.
- Consolidate coverage into the existing 34 destination briefs (10 existing routes, 24 proposed). Proposed destinations are conditional, not an obligation to publish every page.
- Prioritize measured topic demand, distinct user intent and demonstrated product fit. Google keyword volumes are dated estimates, not AI question frequencies; do not sum overlapping phrases as unique demand.
- Answer related questions naturally within worked examples, instructions and concise FAQs. Split a page only when a different intent and substantial evidence justify it.
- Demonstrate actual workflows before claiming capabilities. The 105 evidence-flagged questions are research gates, not a blanket ban on general educational answers.

## Publishing sequence

1. Verify the first workflows and relevant product facts: input materials, commands, generated assets, revisions, final export, observed time/cost and limits. Check crawlability/indexing and maintain accurate pricing, Mac requirements and integration explanations.
2. Improve `/for/reels`; create `/script-to-video` and `/edit-videos-by-chatting`. These three destination briefs cover 55 questions, with one held for specific evidence. Coverage counts are not demand estimates.
3. Expand to captions and YouTube Shorts when demonstrations support the claims; then consider TikTok, existing-footage and other mapped workflows.
4. Refresh competitor comparisons with current sources and equivalent-task tests. Do not portray competitors as lacking modern AI capabilities without evidence. Hold automated long-to-short clipping, dubbing, avatar and other unverified product claims.

Each acquisition page should provide a direct answer, who the workflow fits, a real input-to-export example, representative edit requests, visible output, constraints, relevant pricing context and a clear next step. Connect supporting documentation with internal links rather than duplicating facts everywhere.

## Measurement decision: consumer websites first

Track ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Grok, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode separately. Use fresh conversations and the available private/temporary mode; record date, mode, account state, search behavior and location uncertainty. Private browsing does not prove complete absence of personalization.

The user agreed that the initial browser pilot is sufficient to begin improving pages. We do not need duplicate API-model tests. Keep DataForSEO for keyword research and optional large-scale scraping; model API results and scraped/observed website answers must never be merged into one unlabeled visibility score.

The completed pilot has 50 planned cells (10 questions × 5 surfaces), 43 captured answers and 7 quota-blocked cells: six Grok weekly-limit cells and one Perplexity free-limit cell. No Woven mention appeared in the 43 answers; no woven.video link appeared among captured visible links. This is one sample, not proof of universal absence. Google geography was uncontrolled, and source capture is not an exhaustive inline-citation audit.

Add browser checks as new page topics are developed. Seek representative coverage of all 34 topics over time, without making all 500 questions a prerequisite to publication. Repeat fixed questions after publication and indexing, on different days. Fill quota-blocked cells when access permits. No recurring automation has been scheduled.

Measure separately:

- Whether an answer appeared and whether the page is indexed/discoverable.
- Woven brand mentions, explicit recommendations and cited Woven URLs.
- Which competing products and supporting pages/posts are used.
- Qualified visits, downloads/signups, activated users and paid conversions where analytics supports attribution.

Blocked requests are missing data, never negative recommendations. Informational prompts may legitimately yield no product recommendation. Keep strategic Mac/chat probes separate from stronger-demand topic questions when interpreting results.

## Distribution

Use actual source evidence to identify relevant tutorials, reviews and comparisons. The pilot showed YouTube demonstrations in Google answers and official feature pages in ChatGPT answers; this motivates experiments, not a claim that either channel is universally best. Seek honest hands-on coverage after the demos are ready. No outreach, posting or publishing has been authorized or performed by this strategy document.

## Costs and scope

The consumer pilot made no additional paid API calls; it used existing account quotas and agent time. Earlier $25–50/$75–150 full-bank API estimates were provisional and should not be treated as a budget or quote. A complete 500-question pass across five surfaces is 2,500 checks; three passes are 7,500. Neither is required before the first pages, and no such full run is currently committed.
