# Woven search-demand research
Research date: September 7, 2026. Market: United States. Language: English. Source: DataForSEO MCP, Google Labs keyword database and live Google desktop SERPs.

## Decision
Prioritize (1) AI Reels creation, (2) script-to-video workflows, and (3) ChatGPT-style video editing. Preserve AI video editor as the broad category. Use Mac as a differentiator and compatibility qualifier, rather than limiting acquisition to Mac-specific queries.

These are recommended experiments, not guaranteed rankings. Product fit is based on Woven's inspected public positioning; the workflows still need real-app demonstrations before making stronger marketing promises.

## Method and limits
- Measured 57 seed queries directly.
- Expanded via six category-based keyword-ideas requests and six full-text keyword-suggestion requests.
- Collected 867 keyword rows, representing 796 unique strings. This is a candidate inventory, NOT 796 relevant targets. Many suggestions were unrelated, branded, or informational and were excluded from recommendations.
- Checked five live Google desktop SERPs: ai reels generator, script to video ai, ai clip maker, short form video editor, chatgpt video editor.
- Total returned API cost: $0.27004 across 18 successful paid requests.
- Volumes are provider estimates of average monthly Google searches in the selected market; not users, clicks, forecasts, or ChatGPT prompt volumes.
- Sampled keyword updates were August 2026; latest monthly observations were July 2026. Each raw record retains its update timestamp and monthly series.
- Do not sum similar phrases as unique demand. Synonym filtering does not guarantee audience deduplication.
- KD is DataForSEO's estimated organic ranking difficulty, 0–100. KD 0 is not proof of easy ranking; missing KD is unknown, not zero. Live Reels results include major competitors despite a low estimated KD.
- No Search Console, conversion analytics, user interviews, or AI-answer citation baseline was obtained.

## Live search intent evidence
### AI Reels generator
Results include [Canva](https://www.canva.com/features/ai-reel-maker/), [InVideo](https://invideo.io/make/instagram-reels-maker/), and [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/tool/ai-reel-generator).
The result snippets emphasize generating a complete Reel from a prompt, with media, voice and editing. Free offers are common.
Observed People Also Ask questions:
- Is there an AI that can make reels for me?
- Can ChatGPT make a reel?
- Can I create AI reels for free?
- How do you create AI reels?

Recommendation: answer these naturally with a real Woven demonstration. Do not label the paid app permanently free.

### Script to video AI
Results include [Canva](https://www.canva.com/features/script-to-video/), [Visla](https://www.visla.us/ai-video-generator/script-to-video), [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/tools/script-to-video-maker), and [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/ai/script-to-video).
Intent spans avatar narration, stock footage assembly, and complete narrated videos. Woven's page must show precisely which output it makes rather than treating these as interchangeable.
Useful observed questions: What is the best AI for script to video? Can ChatGPT convert text to video?

### AI clip maker
Results include [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/), [Choppity](https://www.choppity.com/tools/free-ai-clip-maker/), and [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/create/ai/video/clip).
The dominant promise is extraction of short highlights from existing long video, often with automatic reframing and captions. This is distinct from generating new footage.

### Short form video editor
Mixed results include software, tutorials, hiring pages and editing services. Related searches include jobs, Upwork and salary.
Do not interpret all 170 monthly searches as demand for an app.

### ChatGPT video editor
Results include [VEED](https://www.veed.io/tools/video-gpt/chatgpt-video-editing), [InVideo](https://invideo.io/ai/video-gpt/), and [ChatCut](https://chatcut.io/chatgpt-plugin), alongside GPT-editor and discussion results.
This is promising but mixed intent. Explain the integration accurately and show actual actions, rather than just using ChatGPT in a title.

## What not to prioritize yet
- Generic AI video generator (246,000 monthly, KD 65): broad and highly competitive; generation is not equivalent to an editable complete short-form workflow.
- AI voiceover generator (2,400, KD 98): high estimated difficulty and standalone voice-tool intent.
- Faceless terms: candidate demand exists, but branded terms such as faceless.video and free expectations need separate SERP validation before treating them as generic audience demand.
- Hundreds of similar landing pages: overlap does not justify duplication.
- Mac-only keyword targeting: strong positioning can coexist with low exact-query volume.
- Low KD alone: validate intent and competitors before selecting a page.

## Execution and measurement
1. Capture the current Google Search Console baseline by page/query if access is available.
2. Demonstrate one complete Reel and one script-to-video workflow in the current app.
3. Upgrade the Reels page, publish the script workflow, and create a precise chat-editing explainer.
4. Use actual SERP questions for visible answers and FAQs; publish only supported claims.
5. Record 20–25 consistent, unbranded questions across selected AI search products. Measure recommendation presence, citations, and the sources they cite separately from Google search volume.
6. Evaluate impressions, qualified visits, downloads, activated trials and paid conversion by page. Review after sufficient impressions; no arbitrary ranking deadline is promised.
7. Seek independent hands-on coverage once the demos and evidence are ready.
